# Crypto Regime Compounder backtest — 16 July 2026

This report records the independent backtest requested on 16 July 2026. It is a
research approximation of `strategies/crypto_regime_compounder.pine`, not an
export from TradingView's Strategy Tester.

## Configuration

| Setting | Value |
| --- | ---: |
| Chart interval | 1 day |
| Starting equity per symbol | USD 10,000 |
| Commission | 0.1% per fill |
| Slippage | 1 symbol tick per fill |
| Risk per position | 3% of equity |
| Maximum exposure | 100% of equity |
| Maximum strategy drawdown | 25% |
| Weekly cycle EMAs | 10 / 30 |
| Daily trend EMAs | 21 / 55 / 200 |
| Initial stop | 2.8 ATR, bounded to 4–12% |
| Break-even / trailing activation | 8% / 12% |
| Minimum history | 250 bars |
| Partial take-profit | Off |
| Long / short | Both enabled |

Only completed daily candles through 15 July 2026 were used. Orders generated
from a completed bar were filled at the following daily open with adverse
one-tick slippage. Protective stops used daily OHLC prices. Perpetual funding,
bid/ask spread beyond the one-tick assumption, liquidation, taxes, exchange
outages, and webhook latency were not modeled.

drawdown breaker to 35%. Entry and exit logic remain unchanged.

The growth profile crossed 400% only on ETH and required roughly 31% drawdown.
It therefore does not meet a low-drawdown objective and remains research-only.

## Decision

- Keep the balanced profile as the default.
- Advance BTC and ETH to TradingView verification and paper-alert testing.
- Keep XRP and BNB in research until stronger walk-forward evidence exists.
- Reject SOL on the current defaults rather than optimizing specifically for it.
- Keep every recent Delta listing in probation until it has 250 completed daily
  bars, at least two completed positions, liquidity checks, and an untouched
  forward-test period.

The next validation gate is an export from TradingView Strategy Tester using the
same dates and properties. Differences in trade dates, quantities, stops, or
P&L must be reconciled before paper alerts are enabled.
